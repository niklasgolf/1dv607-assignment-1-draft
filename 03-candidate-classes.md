# Task 1 - Candidate Conceptual Classes

After reading through the nouns I found in the problem description, I selected the following as possible conceptual classes.

## People and organizations

Tenant - A person living in an apartment building who can reserve and use laundry rooms.

Property Manager - A person responsible for the buildings, laundry rooms and maintenance.

Maintenance Technician - A person who repairs and maintains machines and laundry facilities.

Housing Company - The organization that owns or manages the apartment buildings and laundry facilities.

Insurance Company - An organization interested in information about serious incidents and damages.

## Buildings and laundry facilities

Apartment Building - A building where tenants live and where laundry rooms are located or connected.

Laundry Room - A shared place where tenants can reserve laundry times and use machines.

Machine - Equipment located in a laundry room.

Washing Machine - A type of machine used by tenants.

Dryer - Another type of machine used by tenants.

## Reservations

Reservation - Represents a tenant's booking of a laundry room for a certain time.

Time Slot - A period of time that can be available or reserved.

Reservation History - Information about previous and upcoming reservations.

## Faults and maintenance

Fault Report - A report created when a tenant finds a problem with a machine or laundry room.

Maintenance - Work performed on a machine or laundry room.

Maintenance Schedule - Planned maintenance for machines or laundry rooms.

Maintenance History - Information about maintenance that has already been performed.

Incident - Something that has happened in a laundry facility, for example damage or flooding.

## Access

Access Permission - Permission for a tenant to enter a laundry room during a reserved time.

Access Attempt - A recorded attempt by a tenant to enter a laundry room.

Access Violation - An attempt to enter or use a laundry room outside the allowed reservation period.

## Notifications

Notification - Information sent to a tenant or another person about reservations or faults.

Reminder - A notification about an upcoming reservation.

## Other possible concepts

Reservation Limit - A rule for how many active reservations a tenant may have.

Maintenance Priority - How important or urgent a maintenance problem is.

Booking Priority - Rules that may decide who can reserve a shared laundry room.
